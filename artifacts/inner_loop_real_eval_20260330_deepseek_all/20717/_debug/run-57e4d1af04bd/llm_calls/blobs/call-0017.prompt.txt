## Scoring Calibration
Avoid the common error of scoring too conservatively. Use the full 1–6 scale:
- A score of 4 means "clear, coherent, and functional" — NOT "excellent." Many competent
  student essays belong at 4, not 3.
- Reserve score 3 for writing that is noticeably limited: predictable ideas, skeletal structure,
  mechanical sentences, or language that rarely engages the reader.
- Reserve score 2 for writing where errors or weaknesses actively impede understanding.
- If the essay is functional and communicates clearly with only minor weaknesses, lean toward 4.
- If you are deciding between two adjacent scores, ask: "Does this essay show control and
  intentionality?" If yes, choose the higher score.
